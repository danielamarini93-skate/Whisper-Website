## How to use this document

This is the complete English copy for every page of the new Whisper website, in page order. Each page lists its SEO title (≤ 60 characters), its meta description (≤ 155 characters), the H1, and then each section with its H2, body copy and CTAs. Form labels are included where a page has a form. The **Target keywords** line under each page shows which search terms that page is written to rank for.

**Tone of voice.** Technical, serious, direct, B2B. Short declarative sentences. Numbers before adjectives. No exclamation marks and no superlatives we cannot prove. Speak to operators, procurement, museum directors, event producers and plant managers, not to end tourists. Use "you" for the buyer and "your guests / visitors / delegates" for the listener.

**Keyword rules.**

- The primary keyword appears in the SEO title, the H1 (or the first H2 when the H1 is a brand line), the first 100 words and the meta description.
- Secondary keywords go in H2s and the body, once each, in natural phrasing. No stuffing and no lists of keywords in the copy.
- Brand terms ("Whisper radio guide system", "Whisper headset", "whisper systems") appear on every page at least once. Never write “tour guide system”: Whisper is not only for tourism, so the product is always a “radio guide” (radio guide system, radio guide headsets, radio guide rental).
- Keep internal links between pages that share keywords (e.g. Rent ↔ Info & Buy on "rental vs purchasing").

**Facts to confirm before launch** are flagged in square brackets, e.g. \[confirm: 90+ languages\].

### The problem

**H2: Multilingual groups without interpreters**

Mixed-language groups usually mean separate tours, extra guides or interpreter booths. Whisper T replaces them with one portable interpretation system that fits in a jacket pocket.

### How it works

**H2: How the simultaneous interpretation device works**

1. **Set up.** In the Whisper Translate app, the operator sets the source language and up to two translation languages.
2. **Capture.** The Beamforming microphone isolates the speaker’s voice, and intelligent noise filtering keeps it clear.
3. **Translate.** AI interpretation converts the speech in real time, with clarity, accuracy and the speaker’s intent.
4. **Listen.** Each listener hears their language on a Whisper X or Y receiver. INFINITY connects them automatically, with no set-up.
5. **Reply.** When a visitor speaks to the guide in another language, Beamforming captures the voice, interprets it and delivers it to the guide.

### Why not consumer translating earbuds

**H2: Real time translation earbuds, built for groups**

Consumer translating earbuds and language translator earbuds work one-to-one and need a phone for each user. Whisper T works one-to-many: one transmitter, up to 3 languages at once and 100+ listeners \[confirm\], with no phones, no pairing and no apps required for the group. Listeners who want to read along can use the optional Whisper Live app.

**H2: Frequently asked questions**

**Rental**

**How do I use a rented radio guide system?** Switch on the transmitter, then the receivers. Receivers tune to the guide automatically. The quick-start card in the case covers each step.

**Where do I collect my rental kit?** At your hotel, office or meeting point, or at a Whisper rental point. The address is in your confirmation email.

**How do I track my delivery?** Every kit is tagged by RFID. You receive SMS and email updates with a tracking link.

**How do I return the kit?** Use the pre-paid return label in the case, or drop it at a Whisper point.

**Devices**

**How long does the battery last?** Whisper X 35 hours, Whisper Y up to 160 hours, Whisper Transmitter 80 hours, Whisper T 6 hours. A full charge takes 1 h for Whisper X on its magnetic base, 4 h for Whisper Y and 1.5–2 h for transmitters by USB-C.

**The receiver can’t hear the guide. What should I check?** Check that the LED shows the connected colour, that the volume is up and that the receiver is within 100 m of the transmitter. Then restart the receiver.
